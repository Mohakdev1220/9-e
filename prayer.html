National & Pledge

Monday & Thursday

O My God, fill the hearts of Thy faithful and enkindle in them the fire of Thy Love. Eternal God, Creator of all - I firmly believe that I am now in Thy presence. I adore Thy Divine Majesty and I ask of Thee to enlighten my mind - that I may know Thee more clearly - and to move my heart - that I may love Thee above all things - and my neighbour for Thy sake. Help me, that I may never in my thoughts, words or deeds offend Thy holy law.

Tuesday & Friday

O my God - at the beginning of this day - I turn to You my Lord and Father - Who looks upon me lovingly as Your child. To You I commit myself and all that is mine that You may protect me from all dangers strengthen me against temptation and keep me away from all evils. Grant me to desire always what is most acceptable to You that Your Will may be mine that my will be conformed to Yours. You alone can help me, deliver me and save me. Accept the offering of all my thoughts, words and deeds. Grant that all my activities may be directed towards You.

Wednesday & Saturday

O God you are the truth and the origin of all knowledge bless our studies - which we consecrate to you. Enlighten our minds, strengthen our memories - direct our wills - towards what is right. Grant us to seek the truth always and make us truly wise. Angel of God, My guardian dear, to whom His love commits me here, ever this day be at my side, to watch and guard, to rule and guide.

Daily Short Prayers

Before Each Period: Oh! My God bless us and enlighten us. After Each Period: Oh! My God we thank you for enlightening us. Before Class: O my God - I offer you today. My work - my lessons and my play. My heart, dear God - I give you. Protect it and keep it true. After Class: We give You thanks / Almighty God/ for all the benefits/You have bestowed upon us / We thank You very specially / for all the new things / we have learnt today / Grant that we may use them / for Your greater glory.

Special Prayers

For Our Benefactors: Reward with eternal life, O Lord, All those who do good to us for Thy name sake. Amen. For the Sick: Lord heavenly Father, [Name] is ailing and suffering from [Illness]. We ask You, Lord, in a very fervent manner to help him/her to bear this suffering patiently and in a spirit of fortitude. You know best what is good for each one of us. Give us the grace to surrender to Your Holy Will. We place our trust in You and pray that with Your Divine help and mercy, he/she will regain normal health. For the Deceased: Lord Heavenly Father, we are very sorrowful at the demise of [Name]. We earnestly implore Your Divine Help for the bereaved members of the family in their hour of trial. Give them the strength to bear their grief at the loss of their dear one. We ask You, Merciful Lord, to grant Eternal Rest and Peace to the departed soul. May Your Perpetual Light shine upon him/her.

A Prayer for Educators (Parents & Teachers)

O Lord, give us the wisdom to deal with our children as You would. To see in each of them Your holy image. To develop in them a genuine love for all men, not only a select few. To nurture in them a divine desire to 'put in' and not simply to 'take out'. To teach them to be 'go-givers' rather than 'go-getters'. O God, in training these dear ones whom Thou hast entrusted to our charge, help us: To encourage rather than discourage them, To discipline with kindness not softness, To guide them intelligently, not blindly, To coach, not scold, to nudge not nag.
