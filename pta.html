P.T.A. PRIZES

PARENT TEACHER ASSOCIATION AWARDS

Language Excellence

Awarded to a student in each class for highest total (min. 9 grade points) in English, Hindi, and Sanskrit.

Scholarship for Good Application

Granted for classes K.G. to XII for General Proficiency in the preceding academic year.

Faculty Medals

Highest marks in Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams in Senior Secondary Exams.

Proficiency Medal

Awarded to students appearing in the Merit list of the Secondary School Examination.

Note: No student can receive two Scholarships/Cash Prizes simultaneously. The higher value scholarship is retained, others must be surrendered. The Principal's decision is final.
General Conditions for Academic Prizes & Scholarships
Passing in all subjects, regular attendance, and good conduct are indispensable conditions.
Absence from Prize Distribution without intimation results in disqualification.
In case of school withdrawal, the award moves to the next eligible student (except Proficiency prizes).
Scholarships are awarded for one year and can be stopped if conditions are no longer fulfilled.
