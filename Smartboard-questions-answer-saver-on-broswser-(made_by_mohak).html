❓ Help & Features

🎯 Quick Start

Type or paste your text in the left panel. It will instantly appear in the right display panel with your chosen formatting.

💾 Saving Files

Click "Save File" to store text in your browser's local storage. Access it anytime from the "Saved Files" section. Files persist even after closing your browser.

🎨 Customization

  • Themes: Light, Dark, and 5 high-contrast presets for accessibility
  • Fonts: Choose from 5 carefully selected fonts including dyslexia-friendly options
  • Font Size: Adjust from 16px to 96px using the slider

📺 Fullscreen Mode

Click "Full" to enter fullscreen mode for maximum display area. Perfect for classroom presentations.

📊 Statistics

Real-time word count, character count, and file size tracking for your text.

🖥️ Browser Storage

All your settings and saved files are stored locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

📋 Privacy & Terms

🔒 Privacy Policy

No Data Collection: This application does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any external servers. All data remains on your device.

Local Storage Only: All text, files, and settings are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage using IndexedDB and LocalStorage APIs.

No Tracking: We do not use analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts. Your usage is completely private.

No Cloud Sync: Your data is never synchronized to the cloud or any external service.
