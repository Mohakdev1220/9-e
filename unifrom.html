CLASS IX UNIFORM GUIDE

Academic Session 2026-27

BOYS - CLASS IX

Regular Uniform (Monday - Friday)

  • Shirt: Light cream color, tucked into Chocolate Brown trousers.
  • Tie: Chocolate brown with design (Compulsory).
  • Belt: Approved school belt is compulsory.
  • Shoes: Plain black shoes with laces.
  • Socks: Plain dark brown nylon socks.
  • Sikh Students: Maroon mini turban (dasta or patka).
GIRLS - CLASS IX

Regular Uniform (Monday - Friday)

  • Dress: Light cream kurta and chocolate brown salwar with chocolate brown half jacket.
  • Hair Style: Two plaits tied with black hair bands (for long hair).
  • Shoes: Plain black shoes with laces.
  • Socks: Plain dark brown nylon socks.
SATURDAY & GAMES DAYS

House Uniform (Class IX)

  • Track Suit: Dark blue with strips of the respective house.
  • T-Shirts: House T-shirts with school mono imprinted.
  • Shoes: White synthetic/canvas shoes.
  • Hair (Girls): Blue hair bands instead of black.
  • Sikh Students: White mini turban.
WINTER WEAR

Winter Season (Class IX-XII)

  • Coat: Maroon Coat with school mono imprinted over the pocket (Compulsory).
